# Chalk Talk: notes for agents

Read README.md first. Zero-build ES modules; the only dependency is aurora-field (pinned).

## Layout
- `src/engine/` is pure and sport-agnostic: `sim.js` (normalize + compile a play into a timeline; defenders' man/zone/rush jobs), `field.js` (the aurora shape + view + particle budget), `geom.js`, `football.js` (route tree, coverages, QB reads).
- `src/sports/` is data: one pack per sport (`basketball.js`, `flag.js` → flag5/flag7, `soccer.js` → soccer7/soccer9, researched in `research/SOCCER.md`) and its plays. Never put sport rules in the engine.
- `src/ui.js` mounts everything in one element; `src/style.js` is scoped under `.ct`. `src/ai.js` builds the schema and briefing from the sport pack; `src/voice.js` is speech in/out and the offline command grammar.
- `src/guide.js` (positions guide + mini boards), `src/iqmode.js` (CourtIQ / FieldIQ game) on `src/engine/iq.js` (pure: questions cut from library plays, Elo, levels, likeness), `src/stats.js` (play numbers, voice, AI meter), `src/analyst.js` (booth commentary from the engine), `src/learn.js` (voice healing + on-device learning), `src/sound.js` (synthesized cues). Position/IQ data lives in the sport packs (`basketball-positions.js`, `flag-positions.js`).
- `research/` holds the cited glossaries; `npm run glossary` regenerates `src/sports/basketball-glossary.js`.

## Invariants
- X's and O's only, never real player names. Basketball offense = positional number (1 PG … 5 C); football = position letters (QB, C, X, Z, Y, H, RB; defense R, CB, S, LB, N). Defender Xn guards On unless a coverage gives it a job.
- Only the ball holder dribbles, passes, hands off or shoots/throws. `npm test` enforces it for every library play.
- Real pro names appear only as IQ likeness labels (archetypes in the positions files), never on the board.
- Basketball passes are chest or bounce only (`ball.passes`; sim picks bounce for a cutter/roller to the rim or a defender in the lane). `src/filmroom.js` is the film room (engine read instantly, brains fill in per card). Themes: `.ct-light` tokens in style.js; the canvas is inverted for light.
- Screens are a sequence (sim.js `TIMING`, `hung`, the `screens` post-pass): a screener whose teammate runs off it the same beat arrives by 0.42 and sets; the user waits (0.36–0.86). A screen with no target is credited to whoever runs past within 5.5. The user's defender is hung up on the screener's near side (same beat: until the end; set one beat, used the next: on the screen spot until ~0.45, then chases). The bar is drawn square to the runner's heading where they pass closest. Handoffs (`handoffMoment`) happen at the beat's moment of closest approach (`ev.sp`), ball hand to hand; with `sport.dho` (basketball) a receiver with nothing planned runs by hip to hip and turns the corner downhill, and the giver becomes a screen (`r.wall`). `test/screens.test.mjs` guards it for every library play.
- Passes lead the receiver: a cut followed by a catch next beat spills into the catch beat (sim.js `carry`), and the ball flies to the catch point. Defenders line up on their side of the ball (flag `guard`, `unguarded`); basketball help comes only on a real drive (`sport.help`, `helperOf`); flag defenders rally after the catch.
- Voice gets better with use, on the device only: `heal()` fixes mishearings (built-in table + the coach's corrections from tapping the transcript), `recall()`/`teach()` turn a phrase the coach kept into an instant offline shortcut, and every attempt's path/timing/outcome is logged locally (Stats → Voice). `test/voice.test.mjs` is the regression corpus: add every real miss there. Nothing said is sent anywhere except to the AI that draws the play, unless the coach opts in to sharing (`share()`): then kept phrases and fixed words go to `/learn` with saved player names scrubbed on the device; `/lexicon` serves back only what 3+ distinct coaches confirmed (`LIMITS.lexiconMin`), and every device merges it under its own learning.
- The AI meter (`meter` in ai.js) records model, tokens, cache, latency and estimated cost per call in localStorage; prices in `PRICES`. The worker returns `x-ct-in/out/neurons` headers for it.
- Keys never leave the browser except to the provider they belong to. The only server is the free open-model Worker (src/worker), which stores hashed-IP counters, opt-in voice lessons, and anonymous usage events (see below). Player names never leave the device.

## Mind view (V, or More → Mind view)
- `src/engine/mind.js` (pure) + `src/mind.js` (DOM, its own scoped `<style>`): a translucent layer with a chip next to every player (what they're doing, speed, space or their man) and up to 3 live cards (brain + instincts, speed sparkline, position/speed/heading/distance, the beat's action and route progress, autonomy decision, ball/ETA, AI thought; defense: job, man + distance, zone rect, screened, beaten, help, reaction lag; offense: space, openness, lane from the ball, who guards them; football: QB progression and this receiver's read; top 3 `bestReads` with scores), plus a sortable table of everyone. Hidden in the IQ game, sketch and presenter mode (root classes); kept in whiteboard-only. Phones: small chips, the card as a sheet above the tab bar.
- Hooks in ui.js are one line each: `createMind(...)`, `mind.rebuild()` in `load()`, `mind.tick(now)` after `drawInk()` only when on, the `V` key, the control-center switch, `destroy`.
- Cost rules: nothing runs while it's off. `facts()` is cached per sim (WeakMap): brains, jobs, and a track sampled every 0.05 beat for speed/heading/distance (no extra `posAt` at runtime; steps longer than 4 marks per sample are the engine re-placing a player at a seam and count as "snaps", not running). Every frame only chip/leader positions move (read from the ink's hot circles). Numbers update at ~9 Hz from one shared `frame()` (the sim's memoized `posAt`), text written only when changed into nodes built once; `bestReads` (O(n²)) at ~3 Hz and only for open cards; card rects are read only when cards change. Measured 2026-10-30 in Chrome on soccer9 with defense (18 players), 3 cards + table open: ~0.08 ms/frame average, ~0.2–0.3 ms per 9 Hz update; the bar shows it live. `test/mind.test.mjs` runs telemetry over every library play, defense on and off.

## Shorts (C, or More → Make a Short)
- `src/shorts.js` records the play on the board as a vertical video (1080×1920, 30 fps) for YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok: a title card (concept + name, 1.2 s, the title said when there's a cloud voice), the play once at teaching pace with captions of each step's line (word-by-word highlight; O/X ids become what the board shows, so names appear only when `state.showNames` is on), a step counter, the wordmark, a 1.4 s recap, then the end card ("Draw yours", mutaafaziz.com/chalktalk). Text stays in the safe zone (out of the bottom 20% and the right 12%). Own scoped `<style>`; ui.js hooks are one line each: `createShorts(...)`, the control-center tile, the `C` key, `short()` on the mount handle, `destroy`.
- Frames: while recording, `.ct-shorting` makes the stage a fixed 540×960 box (the aurora canvas is 1080×1920 at DPR 2; `quality: 'high'` so the governor can't drop it) with the field in the middle 45%, forced dark; the coach sees the composite canvas as the preview. On the field's own `'frame'` event (same task as the WebGL draw, so no `preserveDrawingBuffer`) the 2D canvas gets the backdrop, the aurora canvas, the ink redrawn from the live SVG (each element's `d`/circle/ellipse/text as Path2D; computed styles read only when an element's class changes; drop-shadow filters become canvas shadows), the labels and thought bubbles, then the captions and cards; frames are pushed at 30 fps with `requestFrame()` on a `captureStream(0)`. If the field isn't animating (reduced motion), a rAF fallback forces a draw (`field.set({})`) and composes. Measured 2026-10-30 in Chrome (M-series Mac, 2× display): ~2 ms per composite on average (max ~45 ms, once), 29–30 fps delivered.
- Audio: the narration `<audio>` (sound.js `player`) is routed once, for good, through Web Audio (`createMediaElementSource` → speakers + a `MediaStreamDestination`) and the chimes are tapped into the same mix (`tapCues` in sound.js). Before recording, every line is synthesized (`ready()` in voice.js) so no step waits on the network. speechSynthesis can't be captured: without a cloud voice the Short is captions only, paced by reading time, and the card says a voice key adds narration. Pacing: `planShort` picks the coach's style (analyst first), then faster movement, then the shorter play-by-play lines, to stay under 60 s; while recording, `sim.hold` keeps each step until its line is done (voice: `speaking()`; captions: reading time; 20 s cap). The board stays quiet (`narrate` off) and gets its own hold, speed and theme back after.
- Output: MediaRecorder, H.264/AAC MP4 first (`pickMime`), else WebM; 6 Mbps. Save (download) and Share (Web Share with the file, when `navigator.canShare({ files })`). Cancel or Esc any time; switching tabs cancels (rAF stops); the board is restored the moment recording ends. One feature event (`short`, `{ ext, voice }`), never the play.
- Verified 2026-10-30 in desktop Chrome (macOS, 2× display): Pick and Roll (captions only) → MP4 H.264 High 1080×1920, 38.5 s, AAC track (chime only); Mesh (flag 5v5, defense on) with a stubbed OpenAI TTS (fetch intercepted, no real call) → MP4, 36.6 s, voice mixed in (mean −19 dB). Not verified: Safari/iOS (MP4 expected), Firefox (WebM), a real cloud voice end to end, a 1× display (the field is upscaled 2× there).
- `test/shorts.test.mjs`: layout and safe zones, caption tokens/wrapping/pages, word timing, the timeline (every library play, defense on and off, voice and captions), the format and file name.

## Device layouts (phones first)
- The field is centered in `.ct-boardbox` (aurora-field's `stage`, inside `.ct-stage`); by default it's the whole stage, so desktop and iPad are unchanged. A layout that gives the board only part of the screen moves the box in CSS and sets `--ct-fit: <w> <h>` on it (radius = min(w·box width, h·box height), read by `fitR` via `board.fit()` in ui.js). A ResizeObserver re-lays the field out when the box moves without a window resize. All rules are in the "device layouts" block at the end of style.js, prefixed `.ct:not(.ct-board)` (keeps whiteboard-only as is and outranks the generated `.ct.ct-touch` copies).
- iPhone portrait (375–440 wide): the dock spans the width under 420px (it ran off a 375 screen before), step buttons are 44px, small header pills and close buttons get 44px hit areas (`::after`), the live words sit above the step controls.
- Phones on their side (`@container (orientation: landscape) and (min-width: 480px) and (max-width: 1100px) and (max-height: 500px)`): slim header, the dock becomes a rail on the right (safe-area right), the play chip, caption, step controls and text box in a left column (safe-area left), the card as a side sheet, the field in the middle. In the IQ game the question sits in the left column and the board takes the rest, so no player hides under it.
- Flip cover screens (`max-width: 300px`, or ≤420 × ≤360): sports pills, the play chip, the board, the step controls, and a dock of just Talk + More; Plays and Film room move into the control center (`mini` in `controlCenter()`), no typing, no sketch button. A square cover also drops the caption.
- Folds and two screens: `src/devices.js` `watchDevice(root)` sets `ct-split-x` (side by side: Surface Duo double-portrait, a Fold in book posture) or `ct-split-y` (stacked: Duo on its side, a Fold/Flip half-folded on a table, Flex mode) and `--ct-fold-a/-b` (hinge start/end in px within the root) from `viewport.segments` / `visualViewport.segments`, the `(horizontal|vertical)-viewport-segments: 2` and `(device-posture: folded)` media queries, and `navigator.devicePosture`. Event-driven only (media/posture `change`, `resize` only where segments exist), never per frame. Without script geometry, CSS falls back to `env(viewport-segment-*)`, then 50%. split-x: board alone on the first screen; header, inline play card, dock, sheets and panels on the second. split-y: board on top; header, chip, dock, card and panels below. A pane under 260px, or a board that doesn't span the hinge (embedded), doesn't split. `test/devices.test.mjs` covers the geometry and the watcher.
- Checked in Chrome with same-origin iframes at exact sizes (`ct-touch` added by hand; split classes and `--ct-fold-*` set by hand to emulate segments/posture): 375×667, 390×844, 402×874, 430×932, 440×956 and their landscapes, 260×512 and 260×270 covers, 1114×720 / 720×1114 Duo, 412×915 Flex mode, plus 1440×900 and iPad 820×1180 / 1180×820. Real `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, posture and segment events need the hardware.
- Liquid Glass: one material for everything floating (style.js end): `--glass-clear` (dock, chip, pills, rail, toasts, edge tabs, live pill, presenter bar) and `--glass-regular` (panels, card, IQ HUD, football tools, coach notes), `--glass-blur` (blur + saturate + brightness that dims the aurora under dark glass and lifts it under light), `--glass-edge` (top specular line) and `--glass-depth`. Light mode swaps the tokens; `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast: more` and no backdrop-filter fall back to near-solid. index.html: `viewport-fit=cover`, theme-color per color scheme (ui.js updates every theme-color meta when the coach picks a theme), and the html background follows the board's theme so iOS 26 Safari's floating glass toolbars sit over the right color.

## Drawing accuracy (`npm run eval`)
- `research/eval/plays.json`: 18 coach descriptions (clean, messy speech-to-text, edits of the board, defense, long motion, vague requests, flag coverages) with checkable facts: every expected action in order (who, kind, target), the concept, start/end spots (5 ft / 3 yd) and route depths. `CT_EVAL_KEY=… npm run eval <label>` writes `research/eval/results-<label>.json`; `CT_EVAL_MODEL` forces a Claude model, `CT_EVAL_COACH` a lens, `CT_EVAL_ONLY` a subset.
- Haiku (the free path): 98.1% (17/18) with thinking, median ~9.8 s; thinking disabled (`CT_EVAL_NOTHINK=1`) is 2× faster (5.0 s) but 96.2% (15/18). A library worked example in the prompt made it worse (97.5%, 13.5 s); reverted. Measure before shipping any of these.
- 2026-10-30: Sonnet 5.5 99–100% (17–18/18), ~8 s; Haiku 5.5 96% (13/18), ~13 s. Run it before and after any prompt or schema change; add every real miss as a case.
- Coach lenses (`src/sports/basketball-coaches.js`) shape names, beats, reads and cues ("inspired by" a coach's public system; never quotes or claims to be them).

## The free AI (src/worker)
- **Shared answer cache** (`ai_cache` in D1, `cacheKeyFor`): every successful answer is kept 30 days under a SHA-256 of task + full system prompt + schema + the question. Fresh plays key on the normalized words (any board); edits (`EDITY`) and sketches key on the exact board; `FRESHY` words (another/different/new…) are never cached. Hits skip the AI and the caps (`x-ct-cache: hit`, daily `cache:<day>` bucket on the dashboard). Only hashes are stored as keys; values are plays/answers (X's and O's). A prompt change invalidates by itself.
- **Claude Haiku 5.5 first** (since 0.15.0) when the Worker has an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` secret: `claudeAsker`, structured outputs, cached system prompt, exact cost in micro-dollars tracked in D1 bucket `usd:<day>` against `LIMITS.claudeMicrosPerDay` ($5/day, ≈ 3,000 plays at ~$0.001 each). Past the cap, on any Anthropic error, or with no key: the open-model chain below, unchanged. Per-IP daily caps apply either way. `/health` reports `haiku_usd`, the admin dashboard shows it.
- `/chalktalk/api/*` runs the Worker (`assets.run_worker_first`); everything else is static assets. digitalcraftai.com proxies `/api/chalktalk/*` here (a Vercel rewrite in digital-craft-architect `vercel.json`). The client picks the base in `src/ai.js` (`setApi`).
- Prompts, schemas, validation and the repair round come from `src/ai.js` (`playRequest`, `smartRequest`, `brainRequest`, `solve`), so the free path and BYOK draw the same plays.
- **Models (benchmarked 2026-10-30 on real calls: the DHO description, a flag5 trips call, a sketch takeover, smart players; brains: 12 parallel smart/film calls on pick-and-roll and smash):**
  - play/smart: `@cf/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it` (thinking off). 10/12 clean, the rest usable after normalize. p50 ≈ 20–30 s, ~5k in / ~1.1k out tokens ≈ 80 neurons a call. Falls back to `@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast` (4/4 clean but 18–48 s and ≈ 380 neurons).
  - brain: `@cf/zai-org/glm-4.7-flash`. 12/12 valid, p50 7.6 s, all 12 in parallel done in 17 s, ≈ 35 neurons. Falls back to Gemma (12/12, p50 14 s, ≈ 43).
  - Rejected: llama-3.1-8b (5/12 brains), qwen3-30b-a3b (0/4 plays), gpt-oss-120b (slow, 60–140 s), llama-4-scout (12/12 brains but ≈ 170 neurons and a 90 s straggler). GLM on full plays was 7/8 but slower and mislabeled concepts.
  - hear: `@cf/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo` (~2 s; transcribed "O1 … 5 sets the ball screen" exactly). say: `@cf/myshell-ai/melotts` (~2 s, returns WAV; cached 30 days by text hash).
- **Caps** (`LIMITS`): per hashed IP per UTC day: 40 play, 20 smart, 200 brain, 60 hear, 150 say. Globally 3,500 neurons a day (estimated up front, then corrected to tokens × price), which leaves the shared 10k free allowance room for COLD GAS and Hearth. That's roughly 40 free plays, or ~100 brains, a day; a 429 tells the coach to add a key. Behind the Vercel proxy the per-IP bucket uses the forwarded client IP.
- D1 `chalk-talk` holds only hashed-IP counters (provisioned by name on first deploy). Never log request text or audio.
- `src/server.js` (`chalk-talk/server`): `requestFor(task, body)` + `solve` for any server that runs the AI itself; the Worker and CourtIQ's `/api/labs/chalk-talk/[task]` route (paid plans, CourtIQ's own key) both use it. `mount({ api })` moves only the AI tasks; hearing and the lexicon stay on `ownApi()`. A host answers 402 for "not on your plan" (code `upgrade` → AI settings with the host's `upgrade` button).
- `src/history.js`: My plays (every AI play with the words that made it; repeats on the same board, or a fresh non-edit request, are answered from it with no AI) and `aiCache` (brain/analyst answers by request hash). Voice lines persist in Cache Storage (`chalktalk-voice`).
- Chalk Talk AI is sold through CourtIQ (`/labs/chalk-talk/ai`): the standalone board links a CourtIQ account with a token in the URL fragment (`#ct-link=`), stored as `chalktalk.link`, sent as Bearer only to CourtIQ. A host 402 `{fallback: true}` (monthly cap) silently retries on the free API.
- The view is clamped (`sanePose`): never upside down; the render loop catches per-frame errors so the board can't freeze.
- Analytics (`src/track.js` → `/event`, rollup `/stats` for `admin/index.html` at /chalktalk/admin): cookieless, no IPs, a visitor hash of (day, IP, UA) that rotates daily, country/region/city from Cloudflare, device class, browser/OS family, host, referrer host, sport, AI kind, and feature events with small props. Never names, words, keys. Off on localhost and with `mount({ analytics: false })`. `/stats` needs `Bearer STATS_TOKEN` (Worker secret; the owner's copy is in the gitignored `.stats-token`).
- `/tutor` (IQ tutor, age band only, never a nickname) and `/analyst` (booth lines) run on the brain chain. `/hear` gets a sport vocabulary `initial_prompt` (`?sport=`).
- Tests: `test/worker.test.mjs` (stub AI + D1). Live checks used a route-less preview Worker (`BENCH=1` honours an `x-ct-model` header to force a model); it was deleted afterwards.

## Ads (owner decision 2026-10-30)
- Mutaaf decided on 2026-10-30 to monetize the whole collection through Marquee (mutaafaziz.com/ads; contract in the marquee repo's AGENTS.md), split by audience. Chalk Talk is general audience.
- Only `index.html` (the standalone page) loads `/ads/v1/ads.js` (`data-app="chalktalk"`, slot `library:banner:menu`; Marquee contexts are only `menu`/`gameplay`). It appends the slot plus an "Ads & privacy" link (https://mutaafaziz.com/ads/privacy/) under the Plays list (`.ct-plays` desktop panel, `.ct-playgrid` phone sheet).
- Never put ads in `src/` (the package CourtIQ mounts), on `admin/`, over the board, or while the coach is drawing or speaking. No interstitials: there's no share/export moment yet; if one is added, cap it there, never mid-draw.
- `sw.js` never caches `/ads/*`. `test/ads.test.mjs` guards all of this. Chalk Talk's own cookieless analytics (`src/track.js`) are separate and untouched.

## Environment nuances
- `npm run up` installs (idempotent) and serves on **8795** (8790 is taken by another local server). The dev server maps `vendor/aurora-field.mjs` to `node_modules`; the build copies it.
- Deploy: `npm run deploy` (local wrangler auth). The Worker route `mutaafaziz.com/chalktalk*` beats the site's Custom Domain, like hearth/coldgas.
- aurora-field keeps every shape a field has shown; the UI recreates the field every ~24 plays to bound memory. A `field.remove()` upstream would make that unnecessary.
